    How to Play

    1. Swipe the arrow. When a block reaches the glowing line, swipe across it the way its arrow points. Hit right on time for Perfect.
    2. Smiley blocks can be swiped any direction, or just tapped.
    3. Leave bombs alone. Swiping a spiky red bomb costs glow and breaks your combo.
    4. Lean past walls. Hold the Lean button on the open side (or A and D on a keyboard) until the wall passes.
    5. Chain hits to grow your multiplier up to x8. Your glow bar is your health. If it empties, the song stops.

    Keyboard: arrow keys swipe, Space hits smiley blocks, P pauses. Mouse: click and drag.
